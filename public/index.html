About the bot

HannoGeoBot keeps chat fun and rewards the people who hang around. It runs games, tracks stats, and hands out points that can be spent on rewards or gambled away with the commands below.

Follow (1,000 points) Earn points for following the channel.
Subscribe (5,000 points) For subscribing, resubscribing, or gifting a sub.
Raid (3,000 points) Earn points for raiding the channel.
Redeem First (1,000 points) Be first in chat to claim the bonus.
Watch (5 points every 5 minutes) Keep watching the stream to earn points.
